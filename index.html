FAQ

Ask it straight.

Who is this for?

Homeowners in Odessa and Midland. Property managers and light commercial accounts, when the job is service on a system already in place.

What do you service?

AC and heating repair, diagnostics, maintenance and tune-ups, and system replacement or install for existing homes. Emergency calls when the system is down: call and ask what we can take.

How does a service call work?

You call. We diagnose the system. You get an upfront recommendation. Then we do the fix, or we plan a replacement.

Do you offer tune-ups?

Yes. Call to schedule a tune-up for the heating or the cooling already in the house. (432) 614-1927.

What are signs I should call?

Warm air when the cooling should be on. Heat that will not start. Weak airflow, strange noise, water at the unit, or rooms that never catch up.

Where do you work, and when are you open?

Odessa, Midland, and surrounding West Texas. The shop is at 6820 Austin Ave, Odessa, TX 79762. Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm.
